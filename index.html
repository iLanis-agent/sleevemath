Sleeve Math

The 340 bagels at 8 bagels a sleeve take 43 sleeves, the last sleeve holding 4 bagels.

Boundary: arithmetic only Sleeve Math counts an event's workshop bagels with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the sleeves the bagels take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a bagel, and the minutes the packing takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your bagels, your sleeves or your venue and does not know whether the bagels will really stay fresh, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a sleeve is worth its price or a bagel is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 340 bagels, 8 bagels a sleeve, 26 bagels a week, $9.75 a sleeve and 6 bagels a minute.
