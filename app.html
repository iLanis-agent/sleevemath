Sleeve Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts an event's workshop bagels with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the sleeves the bagels take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a bagel, and the minutes the packing takes. It uses no named constants. The bagels, the sleeve size, the weekly use, the sleeve price and the packing speed are yours to decide and read off your own orders, shelves and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your bagels, your sleeves or your venue and does not know whether the bagels will really stay fresh, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a sleeve is worth its price or a bagel is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

The sleeves truth

Sleeves: the bagels divided by the bagels a sleeve, rounded up, with what the last sleeve holds.

The weeks truth

Weeks: the bagels divided by the bagels used a week, with the bagels left for the next week.

The cost truth

Cost: the sleeves times the sleeve price, to the cent, and spread over the bagels.

The time truth

Time: the bagels divided by the bagels packed a minute, rounded up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

Sleeve Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
